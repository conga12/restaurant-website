Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

Can I make a reservation online?

Yes! You can book your table directly through our online reservation system. We also accept phone reservations for large parties.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We accommodate dietary restrictions, allergies, and special requests. Please mention them when making your reservation so our chefs can prepare accordingly.

Is private dining available?

Yes, we offer private dining rooms for special events. Please contact our reservations team at (123) 456-7890 for more information and availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance to receive a full refund of any deposit. Tables are held for 15 minutes past reservation time.

Do you offer catering services?

Yes! We provide catering services for corporate events, weddings, and special occasions. Contact our chef's office for a custom quote.

Do you have a dress code?

We recommend smart casual or business attire. For special events, please check with us directly. Jackets are suggested but not required.
